Key finding

The top 2 hubs — Kempegowda Bus Station and Krishnarajendra Market — together serve 2,032 routes, nearly 48% of the entire network. Meanwhile, 798 stops are reachable by exactly one route.


This concentration creates a fragile hub-spoke topology. If either major hub faces disruption, roughly half the city's bus connectivity is affected.
