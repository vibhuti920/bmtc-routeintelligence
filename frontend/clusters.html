How clustering works here

Method

Features used

zone_enc — which zone the route passes through
type_enc — Ordinary / Special / Express / etc.
name_length — length of route_long_name
origin_freq — how many routes share this origin
dest_freq — how many routes share this destination
is_premium — Vajra / Volvo / Airport / Express flag

Why K=4

The elbow method on inertia scores across K=2 to K=9 shows the sharpest bend at K=4. Beyond 4 clusters, inertia reduction becomes marginal while interpretability suffers.


The 4 clusters map cleanly to real BMTC network structure: peripheral special routes, central ordinary routes, central variants, and the Shivajinagar secondary hub corridor.
