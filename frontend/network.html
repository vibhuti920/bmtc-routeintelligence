Interpretation

In this network, each stop is a node and each route is an edge connecting its origin to its destination. PageRank ranks stops by how structurally important they are — not just how many routes they have, but how many important stops connect through them.

A stop with PageRank score 10× higher than average is a critical failure point — removing it from the network disconnects many other stops from each other. These are the hubs BMTC should prioritise for redundancy planning.
